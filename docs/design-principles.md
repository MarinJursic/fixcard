# Design principles

1. Preserve evidence; do not generate another guess.
2. Storage scope is explicit: repository, clone-private, or user-global.
3. Human confirmation is explicit; successful commands do not prove causality.
4. Precision at rank one matters more than returning something.
5. A card is untrusted documentation, even after review.
6. Commands are rendered for review and copying, never executed.
7. Clone-private capture is the default; team and global scope are deliberate.
8. No ordinary command pays background, network, or interception overhead.
9. Provenance, mismatch, staleness, and risk appear before instructions.
10. Repeated card use is a prompt to remove the underlying failure.
11. The open format outlives this implementation.
12. Scope is a quality attribute: no history database, agent, chat, cloud sync,
    runbook engine, or observability platform.
